About GV Plus

GV Plus is a modern B2B wholesale platform offering homeware, thermoware and water purification solutions.

As the online evolution of Gruha Varthaka, GV Plus combines decades of wholesale expertise with modern digital commerce.

Gruha Varthaka

Founded in 2009 by Tunk Murali Avadudwala, Gruha Varthaka has become a trusted wholesale supplier of Milton homeware, Thermosteel products and customized gifting solutions across Telangana.

GV Hydro Solutions

Founded in 2025 by Suraj Tunk, GV Hydro Solutions specializes in PP String Wound Filters, RO systems and commercial water treatment solutions.

Our Vision

To become Telangana's most trusted wholesale procurement platform for homeware, gifting and industrial water solutions.
